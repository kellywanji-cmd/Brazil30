17Sunday Búzios

Land and melt into Búzios

The plan

Private car from Galeão to Vila d’Este: about 2½–3 hours and £135 (US$180) per car. Book it ahead, or ask the hotel whether they can arrange it.

  1. 06:50Land. Immigration and bags take about an hour.
  2. 08:00Driver meets you. Sleep in the car.
  3. 11:00Arrive at Vila d’Este. Check-in is from 15:00, so drop your bags, have lunch and wait by the heated infinity pool.
  4. 15:00Into the room for a proper nap.
  5. 17:45Slow walk along the Orla Bardot below the hotel as the boats come in.
  6. 18:40Sunset from the pool deck, then dinner at the hotel’s Altto restaurant over Armação Bay, or crêpes at Chez Michou a few steps away on Rua das Pedras. Early night.

Email the hotel your arrival time (about 11:00) and ask for early check-in, in case the room is ready sooner.

18Monday Búzios

Boat day

The plan

Schooner cruise from the Orla Bardot pier past a dozen beaches and islands: about 2½–3 hours with three 25-minute stops, roughly £10 (R$75) each.

  1. 09:00Breakfast. Book the boat through the hotel or at the pier.
  2. 10:30Board. At the stops Biyi can jump in; you can stay on deck or float by the ladder in a life jacket. The boats carry life jackets and floats.
  3. 13:30Lunch on the Orla Bardot, right by the pier.
  4. 15:00Pool and a nap through the hottest hours.
  5. 20:00Dinner on Rua das Pedras, browsing the boutiques on the way. Book: it’s peak season.
Extras 2 options
  • Catamaran instead of the schooner: more space and shade, £18 (R$130), 11:30–15:00.
  • A private boat (lancha) for just the two of you: ask the hotel for a quote.
19Tuesday Búzios

Beaches by buggy

The plan

Buggy tour with hotel pickup, about 1 hour 20: open-top along the peninsula, with photo stops at Ferradura, Forno, Brava, Tartaruga and João Fernandes.

  1. 09:30Pickup. Book through the hotel the day before.
  2. 11:00Get dropped at João Fernandes: clear, shallow water you can stand in, and kiosks that rent chairs and umbrellas. Lunch on the sand.
  3. 15:00Back for the infinity pool and steam room; pack before dinner.
  4. 17:30Taxi to Porto da Barra in Manguinhos, a waterfront cluster of restaurants, for sunset over the bay and dinner.
Extras 3 options
  • Tartaruga: calm, clear water and good snorkelling for Biyi.
  • Ponta do Pai Vitório: a 10-minute trail to a hidden viewpoint at the end of Praia Rasa. Guided walks from about £18 (US$24) each.
  • Rocka on Praia Brava: clifftop seafood lunch with big views. Brava is a rough surf beach, so it’s one to look at.

If the buggy can’t drop you off, João Fernandes is a short taxi from town.

21Thursday Rio

Christ, the tram and Santa Teresa

The plan

Christ the Redeemer by the Corcovado cog train: 20 minutes up through the forest. Book a timed seat at tremdocorcovado.rio, about £15–18 (R$110–135) each.

  1. 08:30Uber to Cosme Velho station, about 25 minutes.
  2. 09:00Train up. They run every 20 minutes from 08:00; the first one is now the busiest, and the summit often clouds over in the afternoon.
  3. 11:30Uber to the tram station by Largo da Carioca and ride the yellow bondinho up into Santa Teresa: under £3 (R$20) return, 08:00–17:00.
  4. 12:30Lunch with a view at Aprazível (Uber to the door; the lane is steep), or feijoada pastéis at Bar do Mineiro.
  5. 14:30Uber to the foot of the Selarón Steps for photos, then back to the pool.
Extras for the evening 2 options
  • Rio Scenarium in Lapa: three floors of samba, gafieira and choro in an antiques-filled mansion. Doors 18:00, stage 20:00, cover about £4–8 (R$30–60). Reserve a table.
  • San Omakase in Leblon, Biyi’s pick: an 8-seat Michelin-starred omakase counter, about £92 (R$680) each. Book on Tock.
22Friday Birthday

Kelly turns 30

The plan

Sunset on Sugarloaf, then birthday dinner at Oro.

  1. 10:00Slow birthday breakfast and the pool.
  2. 16:45Uber to Praia Vermelha, the little beach at the foot of Sugarloaf, for a coconut on the sand.
  3. 17:30Sugarloaf cable car, two stages up: about £28 (R$205) each; buy timed tickets online. Sunset is about 18:40, then the city lights come on.
  4. 19:15Back to change.
  5. 20:45Oro, Leblon: two Michelin stars, Brazilian produce cooked over fire. Tasting menus £132–149 (R$980–1,100) each, plus 10% service.
Extras 3 options
  • Fancy a walk instead of the first cable car? The Morro da Urca trail starts from the Pista Cláudio Coutinho: free, about 1.9 km and 30–45 minutes through forest. Buy the rest of the ride at the Morro da Urca ticket office, open until 19:30.
  • Helicopter from the Lagoa heliport over the Christ and the beaches: from about £88 (R$650) each for 7–8 minutes; 20-minute loops are about £185. Mornings are clearest.
  • An afro-house or amapiano night, if one lines up. Check RA, Shotgun and Sympla in early January; Afrogroove in Gamboa and Casa Black Rio are the names to watch.

Book Oro as early as they’ll take it, say it’s a 30th, and ask whether they do a non-alcoholic pairing. Plan B: Lasai in Botafogo, Rio’s other two-star, about £169 (R$1,250) each.

23Saturday Rio

Beach, then the Sambadrome

The plan

Free Sambadrome rehearsal from 20:00. União de Maricá, Beija-Flor and Paraíso do Tuiuti run their parades on the real avenue (drums, singers and thousands of dancers, without the full costumes and floats) while you watch from the stands.

  1. 10:00Ipanema beach, around Posto 9 or 10. Rent chairs and an umbrella from a barraca; buy mate gelado and Biscoito Globo from the vendors. The waves and currents here are strong, so paddle only near a lifeguard post.
  2. 14:00Lunch, then a proper nap.
  3. 18:00Early dinner.
  4. 19:15Uber to the Sambadrome, about 30 minutes. Entry is free while sectors have room, so don’t be late.
  5. 20:00The schools start. Stay as long as you like, then Uber home from a busy, lit spot; expect surge pricing.
Extras 3 options
  • A one-hour samba class in the afternoon so you can join in (Airbnb Experiences or GetYourGuide).
  • A morning pre-carnival street bloco if one’s nearby; the 2027 list comes out in early January.
  • Prefer R&B? Swap tonight for Baile Charme under the Madureira viaduct, Rio’s legendary charme party: every Saturday from 22:00 and protected as city heritage. It’s 45–60 minutes by Uber and a late one; do the Sambadrome on Sunday instead.

Take a small towel or cushion for the concrete steps, water and a little cash. Leave bags where you’re staying.

25Monday Rio

Little Africa and Pedra do Sal

The plan

Samba at Pedra do Sal from about 19:00: a free open-air roda on the old stone steps of Pequena África, one of samba’s birthplaces.

  1. 10:30Fashion run in Ipanema: Osklen and Farm Rio, both Rio labels. Shopping Leblon if it rains.
  2. 13:00Lunch in Leblon.
  3. 15:30Uber to Praça Mauá for Eduardo Kobra’s giant Etnias mural, then the Cais do Valongo, the landing wharf for enslaved Africans and now a UNESCO World Heritage Site.
  4. 19:00Pedra do Sal. Stand at the edges and keep phones away. Uber home around 22:00.
Extras 3 options
  • A morning hike, if either of you fancies one: Dois Irmãos, the classic view over Ipanema and Leblon, about 1.5 km up from Vidigal. Go with a guide; tours run about 4½ hours with pickup. Pedra Bonita is the easier one: about 45 minutes up, with hang-gliders launching beside you.
  • Museu do Amanhã on Praça Mauá, about £7 (R$50). Check it’s open: 2026 listings say Thursday to Tuesday.
  • Samba do Trabalhador at Clube Renascença: Mondays from 16:30, the locals’ afternoon samba.

Rio is 3 hours behind London.

Three nights in Búzios to recover from the flight, then six in Rio, where your birthday, the free Sambadrome rehearsals and the flight home all fall. You change stays once, by private car, and never take a domestic flight.

Búzios is the prettiest beach town you can reach by car alone. Boats and buggies show you a dozen beaches without anyone needing to swim, and the centre is small enough to stroll to dinner.

Rio puts the beach on your doorstep, the Arpoador sunset close by, and the airport an hour or less away for the flight home.
